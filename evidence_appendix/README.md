## Headline results

- **S1:** 18 scenarios (the 17 tests plus a price-rising futures run). Scale requirement met: Margin call $93,700 (30.2%), MOI future, rising path (price-rising run) $97,000 (31.3%) against the 25% threshold of $77,500.
- **S1 traces / E3:** a step trace for all 18 scenarios with time, actor, local knowledge, action, packet/transmission, arrival and financial state after; a ledger of every balance change with owner, location, asset, source, encumbrance and liability; open obligations at every financial step. Cash and shares conserved at every step in 18 of 18 scenarios; over-encumbered holdings: 0.
- **S3:** routing tables at h 0 and h 300 for all nine settlements; value-move timelines for the best, median and worst client: best Ceres complete at h 7.10; median Jupiter complete at h 7.87; worst Neptune complete at h 39.46.
- **E4:** 5748 Sun-closure boundaries refined to 1 s; 5748 flip exactly at the refined time (t − 1 s / t / t + 1 s). Shortest closure 6.68 d vs a 6 h scan step. No settlement ever loses both relays. Hub's longest closure 190.2 d.
- **E5:** 6 epochs (1, 10, 100 years and three difficult epochs chosen from E4); value move and both price directions at each.

## Read before using these numbers

1. **Opening balance sheet.** The engine's opening sheet is $310,000 across 6 accounts; the $50,000 guarantee fund is contributed after hour 0 (E-Alice and E-Bob, $25,000 each), as the brief requires (institutions start with nothing). A design paper that lists the fund as an opening row with a $360,000 total does not match the implementation.
2. **E5 finding (not fixed).** At the 'hub loses a relay' epoch both futures stay open past maturity: the pinned Mars → Ceres session waits through a closure, the maturity mark is abandoned after 4 attempts, and nothing resubmits it. Proactive re-pinning and resubmission by transaction ID are described in the paper but not implemented. See `e5/E5.md`.
3. **E5 checks.** 'shares travelled the pinned route' is written for the hour-0 geometry and fails at three later epochs where a different relay is faster; trades, invariants and conservation are unaffected.
4. **Maintenance before hour 0.** Under the flight-overlap rule, Relay B–Neptune launches from about h −2.4 fail; the brief also says maintenance does not apply before hour 0. See `e4/E4.md`.
5. **Conditional traces.** No random loss: losses happen only where a test forces them or an incident covers the launch. Idle-session expiry and link queues are not simulated.
