# E5 · Shifted epochs, including deliberately difficult periods (hub Ceres)

**Reproduce:** `python3 -m evidence.appendix e5` (runs E4 first to choose the difficult epochs) → `e5/e5_epochs.json`, `e5/e5_runs.csv`, `e5/e5_routes.csv`. One epoch alone: `MPEX_EPOCH_OFFSET_H=<hours> python3 -c "from evidence.appendix import e5_epoch; print(e5_epoch())"`.

**Tier claimed: 3** (1, 10, 100 years; three difficult epochs chosen from the E4 scan; value move and both price directions at each).

Each epoch starts every scenario at t = offset; planets and relays are advanced from the original epoch; balances reset to the opening sheet; the original maintenance windows and the S2 incident are not replayed. Margin windows are recomputed from the geometry at the epoch.

## Finding at the difficult epochs

- **hub loses a relay, price falling**: maturity is h 300, but only 8 of 25 price marks reached Ceres Clearing by h 320, and 17 were abandoned after 4 end-to-end attempts (including the maturity mark). The Mars → Ceres session is pinned to Mars → Relay B → Ceres; its Mars–relay link enters a Sun closure while Ceres's other relay is in its 190-day closure. The position is still **open** at h 2880 (run extended to 120 days): funded and owned (calls met 3/3), which the brief accepts as a final state, but it never reaches maturity settlement.
- **hub loses a relay, price rising**: maturity is h 300, but only 8 of 25 price marks reached Ceres Clearing by h 320, and 17 were abandoned after 4 end-to-end attempts (including the maturity mark). The Mars → Ceres session is pinned to Mars → Relay B → Ceres; its Mars–relay link enters a Sun closure while Ceres's other relay is in its 190-day closure. The position is still **open** at h 2880 (run extended to 120 days): funded and owned (calls met 4/4), which the brief accepts as a final state, but it never reaches maturity settlement.

**Why this matters:** an open route existed throughout (Mars → Relay A → Relay B → Ceres; Relay A ↔ Relay B is never blocked). Two behaviours the design paper describes are not implemented: proactive re-pinning of sessions before a predictable closure (paper §5.1) and application resubmission of an abandoned message by transaction ID (paper §12). This is reported as found, not fixed: the brief asks for honest evidence, and no rule was changed to improve the result.
